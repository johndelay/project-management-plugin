The plan's kill criteria contain BOTH (a) an effort trigger tied to the time budget (for example "1.5 times the appetite spent" or "more than N sessions") AND (b) at least one value trigger tied to the success metric (for example "tickets do not fall by X by the re-measure date"), and EACH trigger names the action agreed in advance (stop and ask, revisit, kill). FAIL if either kind of trigger is missing or a trigger has no action.
